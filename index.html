Parameter Definitions

Maximum Flow Rate (Qmax): Peak stormwater runoff rate entering the pond (m³/s). Typical values range from 0.1-5 m³/s depending on catchment size.

Settling Velocity (Vs): The speed at which sediment particles settle in water (m/s). Common values:

  • Sand (0.1-1 mm): 0.01-0.1 m/s
  • Silt (0.002-0.05 mm): 0.0001-0.001 m/s
  • Clay (<0.002 mm): <0.0001 m/s

Pond Depth (d): Design depth of sediment pond (typically 2-4 meters). Shallower depths improve settling but require more area.
